# Business Questions

These are the specific questions this project will answer using real data
(not assumed answers — findings will come from Phases 5-11).

1. Which suppliers contribute the most total emissions?
2. Which suppliers have the highest carbon intensity (CO2e per $ spent)?
3. Which categories have the largest carbon footprint?
4. Are high-spend suppliers necessarily high-emission suppliers?
5. Which transport modes contribute most to emissions?
6. Which suppliers are both high-spend AND high-carbon-risk?
7. Where are the biggest decarbonization opportunities?
8. What happens if we reduce reliance on high-carbon suppliers?
9. What is the potential CO2e reduction under a shift scenario?
10. What is the potential procurement cost impact of that scenario?

## Note
These are investigation questions, not pre-decided conclusions. Findings
in Phase 11 (Business Analysis) will be written only after the dashboard
produces real results.
